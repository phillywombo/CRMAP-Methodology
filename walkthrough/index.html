CRMap

Mapping the U.S. advisory-firm market from public filings, one feature at a time.

Phillip Wombacher · Swift · C · Go · SQL Full methodology →   Download MP4

Independent project built on personal time and equipment using public data only (SEC Form ADV and 13F, IAPD, BrokerCheck, Census, IRS, BLS, CDC, OpenFIGI). Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or used in the business of any employer. Individual adviser names are blurred. Performance figures shown are estimates from disclosed 13F holdings, not actual firm returns.
